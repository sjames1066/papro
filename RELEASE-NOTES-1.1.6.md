# PAPro 1.1.6

## New
- **Pack updates on the Dashboard.** When an installed pack has an update (catalog or Community), a banner with an Update button appears at the top. Updating also applies your flight with the new pack.
- **Release.bat.** One click builds the installer, creates the GitHub release and publishes catalog.json.

## FSLabs
- **Early landing PA.** The After Landing PA is also built on short final (500 ft) with the sim's real local time, then again after landing.

## Aircraft
- **FSS E-Jets (E170/E190):** the beacon is read from the aircraft, so Boarding complete works.

## Dashboard
- The no smoking sign is removed. The seat belt sign stays.

## Fixes
- Pack Studio: exporting a single sound works in PAPro's own window (downloads were blocked).
